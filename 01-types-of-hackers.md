Types of Hackers

Introduction

A hacker is a person who uses technical knowledge to understand computer systems and networks. Hackers are classified based on their intentions and authorization.

1. White Hat Hackers

- Also known as Ethical Hackers.
- Work with proper permission.
- Identify security vulnerabilities.
- Help organizations improve security.

Example: A security professional testing a company's website with authorization.

2. Black Hat Hackers

- Access systems without permission.
- Exploit security weaknesses for harmful or illegal purposes.
- May steal data or disrupt services.

Example: An attacker stealing confidential customer information.

3. Grey Hat Hackers

- May discover vulnerabilities without prior permission.
- Do not necessarily have harmful intentions.
- May report vulnerabilities to the organization afterward.
- Unauthorized access is still not acceptable.

Example: A person finding a website vulnerability without authorization and informing its owner.

Comparison

Type| Permission| Main Purpose
White Hat| Authorized| Improve security
Black Hat| Unauthorized| Harmful activities
Grey Hat| May be unauthorized| Discover vulnerabilities

Conclusion

Understanding hacker types helps us identify the difference between ethical hacking and malicious hacking.

Key Learning: Always perform security testing with proper authorization.
